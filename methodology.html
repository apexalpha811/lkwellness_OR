Fixed overhead totals $355,650 a month. Each line below shows its build-up.

Rent — $36,850/month

6,700 SF × $5.50/SF/month NNN.

This is the most exposed number in the model. There is no broker letter, no comparable set and no listing behind it anywhere in the lineage. The prior Playa Vista model implied $6.77/SF, so $5.50 is the more aggressive figure. Sensitivity is shown at the end of this document.

What the margins pay for

Cost of goods runs $167,781 a month against $650,807 of revenue, a blended 74.2% margin. What they cover:

CategoryMarginWhat the cost side is
Recovery80–85%Consumables, filters and water treatment, linens, device servicing contracts. Almost no per-session variable cost, which is why these margins are the highest in the building.
Regenerative72–78%PRP kits and centrifuge consumables, IV bags, vitamins and NAD+, peptides and pharmacy cost, tubing and sharps.
Aesthetics65–70%Neuromodulator and filler units at acquisition cost, laser tips and consumables, RF and cryo applicators, topicals. The lowest wellness margins because product cost dominates.
Diagnostics62–70%Reference lab send-out fees, DEXA consumables, DNA and DUTCH collection kits, interpretation time.
Surgery — facility fee78–80%Disposables, implants, drugs and linens, instrument reprocessing. Clinical staff sits in fixed overhead and the surgeon takes no share of the facility fee, which is why these exceed the treatment-room lines.
Surgery — full fee73–76%As above, plus graft handling and technician time on hair restoration and laser consumables on ablative resurfacing.

Use of funds, line by line

Buildout and tenant improvements (5,800 SF clinic and club @ $250/SF) — $1,450,000

Square footage × $250/SF for clinic and club space.

The prior Playa Vista model used $200–$300/SF for comparable clinic buildout, so $250 sits mid-range. It is not a contractor bid; a general contractor bid remains the most valuable document to obtain before closing.

Surgical suite buildout (900 SF @ $320/SF, OR-grade HVAC and finishes) — $288,000

900 SF × $320/SF for OR-grade construction.

iOR publishes $120–$220/SF nationally for an office-based surgery suite. $320 sits above that band deliberately: Playa Vista is a high-cost construction market, and positive-pressure HVAC with filtered air changes is not a clinic-grade system.

Medical gas, emergency power, life-safety branch for the suite — $100,000

Medical gas plus emergency power and life-safety branch for the suite.

The only externally cited construction figure in this table. The published ranges are $60K–$80K+ for medical gas and $40K–$100K for emergency power, a combined $100K–$180K. This line sits at the bottom of that range, which is the aggressive end.

Working capital and debt service reserve — $1,395,000

Sized against the modelled cash trough, not set to a round number.

This is the one capital line that is computed rather than estimated. The model runs a 36-month cash curve and this reserve must exceed the deepest cumulative shortfall; a build assert fails if it does not. That is why the figure is not round.
